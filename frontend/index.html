About MachineMatch

MachineMatch is a concept for a B2B machine discovery platform. It helps users find, compare and evaluate used industrial machines based on their individual requirements.

How it works

  1. Search for machines
  2. Apply your requirements and filters
  3. Review the matching machines
  4. Compare suitable options
  5. Contact the seller

The matching score provides a transparent indication of how well a machine matches the selected requirements.

MachineMatch is a portfolio project. All machines, sellers and listings shown on this platform are synthetic demo data.
